Purpose of This Document
The foundational SMPL architecture — SKA, SCBM, SCDM, the CKR Standard, and the ERP, CRM, Billing, and HRIS Connector Knowledge Registries and IMS Libraries — is now frozen. Implementation execution is the next phase of work, and the Customer Environment Profile (CEP) is the primary document that carries a customer through it.
The CEP is the bridge between customer discovery and Financial Intelligence. It captures only what SMPL cannot determine on its own, and it evolves continuously as implementation progresses — it is not a static intake form.
Guiding Principle
If AI can discover it, do not ask the customer.
Design Commitments
Completable by a customer in under 15 minutes on first pass.
Free of technical questions wherever possible.
Never asks for information already available through connected systems.
Clearly distinguishes customer decisions from system configuration.
Usable by both AI implementation agents and human implementation consultants.
Continues evolving throughout implementation as new information becomes available.
How to Read This Document
Every field in the CEP is labeled with one of four types, so it is always clear who — or what — is responsible for populating it:
Field Type
What It Means
CUSTOMER INPUT

Appendix B — Customer Experience Principles
The customer should feel that SMPL already understands their business. Discovery should feel conversational, not administrative.
The customer spends their time confirming business decisions — not describing technical systems.
The CEP behaves as a collaborative implementation document, not a lengthy questionnaire.
Every question the customer sees should be one only they could answer.
If AI can discover it, do not ask the customer.
