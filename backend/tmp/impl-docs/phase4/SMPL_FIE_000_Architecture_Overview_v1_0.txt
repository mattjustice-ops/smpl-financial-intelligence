1. Purpose of the FIE Layer
The Implementation Foundation — Connector Proxy, Orchestration, Canonical, Validation, and Knowledge (BIS-001 through BIS-005), plus Reporting (BIS-006) — answers one question: can we acquire, standardize, validate, and understand a customer's data? The Financial Intelligence Engine answers a different one: given trustworthy data, what does it mean, and what should the organization do about it?
Where the Implementation Foundation ends at trusted Canonical Business Objects, the FIE layer begins there and produces five things no prior layer could: a permanent historical record of what happened and what the organization believed (FIE-001), an explicit, versioned definition of how this specific organization calculates its own numbers (FIE-002), deterministic execution of that definition against that history (FIE-003), a permanent record of the organizational reasoning and decisions surrounding all of it (FIE-004), and evidence-based reasoning that turns all of the above into explanations, findings, and recommendations a finance professional can independently verify (FIE-005).
2. Why the Five Services Exist Separately
Every one of the five answers a genuinely different question. Building them as one monolithic 'Financial Intelligence' service would have collapsed distinctions this platform depends on — most importantly, the boundary between what is a fact, what is a belief, what is a rule, and what is a conclusion.

7. How CSV/API Ingestion Reaches FIE Through Canonical
ABS-012 established the governing principle every FIE service depends on structurally, not just by convention: every implementation path — CSV/Excel upload (Path A), live connector API (Path B), or a per-domain mix of both (Path C) — converges into the same Canonical Business Objects before anything downstream ever sees it. No FIE service, and no BIS-004 through BIS-006 service, has any code path that behaves differently based on how a canonical object was populated.
Concretely: FIE-001's source_system field on a Temporal Record is descriptive metadata, not a behavioral branch — an Observation derived from a CSV-sourced invoice and one derived from a live NetSuite sync are structurally identical records. This is why building five FIE services against Canonical, without reference to any specific ingestion mechanism, turned out to make ABS-012 possible without modifying a single one of them (ABS-012 §11 verifies this document by document). It is also why a customer can start on Path A and later graduate a single domain to Path B without re-implementing anything above Canonical — FIE-001's history simply continues under a new source_system from the graduation date forward.

When a value combines multiple inputs — a formula drawing on an Observation and an Assumption Revision, or a Finding drawing on an FIO and a Knowledge Object — the platform's rule is the minimum across contributing inputs, per dimension, first established in FIE-003 §1.8 and extended to a fourth dimension in FIE-005 §19. A result is only as trustworthy as its least-trustworthy input; nothing is ever averaged into looking more confident than its weakest contributor. Once written, no confidence value on any persisted record is ever recalculated retroactively, anywhere in the platform — confidence is a fact about a moment, not a live-updating score.
9. Explainability Relationship
Financial Explainability — showing the source records, calculation lineage, business context, and confidence behind any conclusion — is deliberately not owned by any single FIE service, and specifically not by FIE-005 despite being its first and most demanding consumer. Every fact Explainability surfaces already has a system of record: FIE-001 for data lineage, FIE-003 for calculation lineage (its own Financial Evidence Package, assembled on read rather than stored separately), FIE-004 for business context, and all four services collectively for confidence context.
At MVP, FIE-005 satisfies its own need for this through an internal Explainability Adapter — a component that implements a fixed five-operation contract (resolve data lineage, calculation lineage, business context, confidence context, and tie-out) by calling the four owning services directly. This is a deliberate placeholder for a future shared Explainability Platform serving FIE-005, Reporting, dashboards, and future agent workflows alike, without duplicating any of the four services it currently calls directly. The contract is fixed specifically so that building the shared version later changes nothing about how FIE-005 asks for what it needs.

Two separate promotion mechanisms exist in the platform, and they move in different directions for different reasons.

How It Reaches FIE-004

Table FIE0-10.1 — A stated asymmetry, not an inconsistency: Recommendations carry governance weight from the moment they exist; Findings may never need to leave FIE-005's own store.
10.2 FIE-004 → BIS-005: Customer Knowledge Into Platform Knowledge
Customer Organizational Memory never automatically becomes reusable platform knowledge. The only path is FIE-004's Promotion Pipeline: Governance Review → Anonymization → Approval → BIS-005. This is never triggered by FIE-004 itself — it requires an explicit request and a human governance reviewer, and only an approved request ever results in a write to BIS-005, which happens through BIS-005's own API rather than a direct table write.
11. How Reporting Consumes FIE Outputs
BIS-006 (Reporting) supplies presentation; FIE-005 supplies content. A Narrative Object is content FIE-005 owns outright — the text, its evidence references, its four-dimensional confidence, all immutable once verified. A rendered board deck, PDF export, or dashboard view containing that Narrative is a BIS-006 artifact that references it. BIS-006 never generates reasoning or narrative text of its own for financial content; it renders and exports what FIE-005 already produced and verified. This mirrors exactly the relationship BIS-006 already has with Canonical, Validation, and CAL data for implementation-stage reporting — Reporting has never, at any layer, been the place conclusions originate.
12. Key Architectural Principles
Principle
What It Means in Practice
Canonical Convergence
Every ingestion path (CSV, API, hybrid, and future mechanisms) converges on the same Canonical Business Objects before any FIE service sees the data — ingestion mechanism is invisible above Canonical
Reference, never duplicate
A fact or value owned by one service is always referenced by another, never copied — Decision Memory references an FIO rather than storing its own copy of the number; a Finding references an Observation rather than restating its value
Immutability with versioning
Nothing in the FIE layer is ever edited in place — a change is always a new version, and history is always reconstructable exactly as it was at any past point in time
Deterministic execution, separate from reasoning
FIE-003 is the only FIE service with no LLM anywhere in its execution path — determinism is achievable at the source there, and bounded through immutable storage everywhere reasoning or narrative generation is involved instead
Fail-closed verification
FIE-005 blocks publication by default on any unresolved evidence, contradiction, or confidence shortfall — there is no override path, only Draft Mode for internal iteration
Promotion is never automatic
Both promotion mechanisms in Section 10 require an explicit, separate, governed action — nothing organizational or customer-specific becomes durable or shared without a deliberate step
Confidence is never collapsed
Four dimensions, always kept separate, combined by minimum-across-inputs when necessary — a result is never made to look more trustworthy than its weakest input

Closing Note
The Financial Intelligence Engine is complete as five services, each answering exactly one question, each depending only on the services before it, and none duplicating what another already owns. That property was not achieved by planning it all at once — it emerged from reviewing each service's conceptual architecture against every service that came before it, catching adjacent-territory conflicts (BIS-005 vs. FIE-002 vs. FIE-004, the Financial Evidence/Reasoning Package overlap, the Findings addressability gap) before they became schema migrations instead of clarifying paragraphs.
This document will go stale the moment any of FIE-001 through FIE-005 changes. It should be treated as a map, not a territory — useful for orientation, never authoritative over the documents it summarizes.
