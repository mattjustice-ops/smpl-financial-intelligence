## 1. Where to look (types of places — not a shortlist)

Use **categories**, then fill names yourself from public sources. Do not treat this as a ranked recommendation.

| # | Place to look | What you usually get | How to use carefully |
|---|---------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| 1 | **Founder / operator referrals** | Warm intros to lawyers who already draft SaaS Order Form + DPA packages | Ask peers who closed similar US B2B SaaS deals; note conflict / capacity |
| 2 | **Startup / tech lawyer networks** | Boutique or small-firm commercial + privacy counsel used to cloud SaaS | Prefer practitioners who say they draft **customer-facing DPAs**, not only NDAs |
| 3 | **State / local bar association lawyer referral services** | Public directory / referral desk for licensed attorneys in your state | Use as a **directory**, not a quality rating — verify practice area yourself |
| 4 | **Law-firm practice pages (tech transactions / privacy / commercial)** | Firms that publicly list SaaS, data processing, or privacy commercial work | Read for fit (stage, US B2B, cloud subprocessors); ignore marketing star ratings |
| 5 | **Accelerator / community counsel desks** (if applicable) | Office hours or preferred-provider lists from programs you already trust | Still your diligence — preferred ≠ engagement |
| 6 | **Prior counsel / accountant intros** | Someone who already knows your entity and can introduce commercial counsel | Useful if entity docs / prior work product already exist with them |

**Explicitly out of scope for agents:** picking a firm, inventing “top 3” names, scraping review sites into a ranked list, or sending outreach.

## 2. What to ask before you engage (short screen)

Copy/adapt; fill blanks yourself.

1. **Scope:** Can you redline / draft a **US B2B SaaS Customer DPA** (and MSA or combined Customer Agreement) suitable to attach to Order Forms?
2. **Independence / packaging:** Are you comfortable drafting from our outline + counsel pack ([COUNSEL_SEND_PACKAGE.md](./COUNSEL_SEND_PACKAGE.md)), or do you prefer your firm’s template first?
3. **Honest certifications:** Will you avoid inventing **SOC 2 / ISO / GDPR certification** claims? (We are **not** SOC 2 certified; Type I readiness only.)
4. **Subprocessors / AI:** Can Exhibit A track our living subprocessor list, and can AI/LLM (Anthropic + P15) be handled as a schedule or DPA section?
5. **Turnaround + fee shape:** Rough ETA for first redline; fixed-fee vs hourly for a first customer-ready draft; who is the day-to-day contact.
6. **Entity / conflicts:** Confirm they can represent your entity; flag any conflict checks early.

Commercial defaults still yours to decide later (deletion window, subprocessor notice/objection, breach notice, liability, governing law) — see counsel pack §7.
