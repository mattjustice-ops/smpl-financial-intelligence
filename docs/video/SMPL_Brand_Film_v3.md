# SMPL.ai Brand Film (v3)

## COMPLETE REWRITE

**Length:** 90 seconds

**Objective**

Do NOT create a software demo.
Do NOT create a feature walkthrough.

Create a cinematic story that every VP Finance, Controller, Head of FP&A, and CFO immediately recognizes.

The viewer should feel: "That's exactly what my finance team goes through every month."

Only after that emotional connection should SMPL.ai appear as the solution.

The pacing should feel premium, modern, and deliberate.
Think Apple. Think Linear. Think Stripe. Not a SaaS explainer.

Product interface appears no earlier than ~0:45.

---

## Brand Guidelines

- Background: Deep navy (#00041A)
- Accent: SMPL teal (#30CFCA)
- Typography: Modern sans serif. Large. Minimal.
- Animation: Slow. Smooth. Purposeful. Never rush.
- Nothing bounces. Nothing spins. Everything feels expensive.

---

## Story Structure

Tell the story of every finance team's month-end.

| Scene | Time | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:12 | Problem: Accounting closes. Finance begins. Quiet stress. |
| 2 | 0:12–0:25 | Why reporting takes weeks — disconnected systems montage |
| 3 | 0:25–0:38 | Frustration — conflicting numbers, silence |
| 4 | 0:38–0:48 | Introduce SMPL — freeze → logo |
| 5 | 0:48–1:05 | Transformation — systems → one operating model |
| 6 | 1:05–1:20 | Why it matters — Copilot / traceable explanation |
| 7 | 1:20–1:30 | Emotional close — board ready, relaxed |

---

## Production Standards

Premium Apple keynote feel. Dark. Elegant. High contrast.
Minimal purposeful animation. Large readable type.
One core message per scene.
Premium warm confident VO — no synthetic-sounding AI voice for final.
No robots, holograms, spinning charts, generic AI graphics.

## Pipeline notes (for production)

- Visuals: Remotion cinematic motion graphics (typography-led + stylized desk/UI environment). Live-action with a real FP&A analyst requires shoot/stock assets — not assumed in v1 brand-film build.
- Music: Needs licensed bed (start at Scene 1 after second title card). Placeholder silence until provided.
- VO: Edge TTS for drafts only; final should use ElevenLabs (or recorded human VO) per Part 3 / Production Standards.
